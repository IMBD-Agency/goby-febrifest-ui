Refund & Return Policy

We offer a transparent 7-day refund and exchange policy for all items.

1. 7-Day Exchange Period

If you're not satisfied with the size, fit, or color of your item, you can request an exchange within 7 days of receiving the package. Ensure the tags are intact and the garment is unwashed.

2. Defective Items

In case of printing errors, fabric damages, or wrong item shipments, we offer completely free pickups and replacements. Please contact our support team with unboxing footage/photos.

3. Refund Processing

Once your returned item passes our quality inspection checks, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days to your Mobile Banking wallet (bKash/Nagad) or Bank Account.
